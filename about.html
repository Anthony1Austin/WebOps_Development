    About WebOps Development LLC

    I own and operate WebOps Development LLC. I partner with businesses to design and build custom websites that load fast, read clearly on every device, and are structured for search visibility—aligned with how you work and what you want visitors to do.

    From discovery through launch, you get direct communication, realistic timelines, and a polished handoff—so your site supports real business goals without unnecessary friction.

    Service Offerings

    1

    Web Design (UI/UX)

    Layout & hierarchy: Clear typography, spacing, and navigation so visitors find what they need quickly.

    Wireframes & prototypes: Structure and content flow are agreed before build, reducing rework and surprises.

    2

    Front-End Development

    Responsive, accessible markup: Semantic HTML and CSS that scale across viewports and support assistive technologies.

    Interactive UI: Components and motion where they improve clarity—without slowing the page down.

    3

    Back-End Development

    Data & logic: Databases, serverless functions, and APIs wired for forms, bookings, and the features your site requires.

    Integrations: Connections to email, CRM, payment, and analytics tools your business already relies on.

    4

    CMS Setup (e.g. WordPress)

    WordPress & themes: Installation, theme customization, and plugin choices aligned with how your team publishes content.

    On-page SEO: Titles, structure, and editing workflows so new pages stay consistent and search-friendly.

    5

    E-Commerce Solutions

    Catalog & checkout: Product presentation, cart, and checkout flows with trusted payment providers.

    Security & operations: SSL, sensible defaults, and hooks for fulfillment and reporting as you grow.
